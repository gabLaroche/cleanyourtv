# Chromecast with Google TV 4K (2020, device sabrina), Google TV 14 (SDK 34), build UTTC.250917.004 (14193937)
#
# Debloat only: the same cleanup as debloat.txt, minus the three packages that cost something on this device.
# Phone casting keeps working, the stock Google TV home stays, and so does the ambient screensaver.
#
# Apply:   TV_HOST=<ip> tools/disable.sh examples/google-chromecast-gtv-4k/debloat-keep-cast.txt
# Undo:    TV_HOST=<ip> tools/restore.sh examples/google-chromecast-gtv-4k/debloat-keep-cast.txt com.google.android.apps.tv.launcherx
# Everything is DISABLED, not uninstalled.
#
# Kept on purpose, unlike debloat.txt:
# - com.google.android.tungsten.setupwraith: it holds the Cast receiver's device credentials (debloat.txt, quirks).
# - com.google.android.apps.tv.dreamx and com.google.android.backdrop: without the new launcher's Clock
#   screensaver, disabling them leaves the screensaver pointing at nothing.
# Move on to debloat.txt when you install the launcher and accept losing phone casting after a reboot, or keep
# this list and take Home with the redirect instead (third unit below, config.json in this folder).
#
# Lived with on 2026-10-22: stock home, phone casting (YouTube), Play Store and Assistant all worked.
#
# Third unit: Canada, same build, 2026-10-25, reached over Tailscale. This list as is, plus seven streaming apps
# the household does not use (not listed); after a reboot the disabled list was unchanged.
# - The launcher on top of it with launcherx and setupwraith left enabled: the keys in config.json, set in
#   config.xml, and `STOCK_LAUNCHER=none HOME_KEY_SERVICE=1 launcher/build-and-install.sh`. The script stops at
#   its Home check, as expected while launcherx is Home; HomeKeyService was then enabled the way the script does
#   it. Home pressed in YouTube landed on the new launcher, and after a reboot the TV came up on it (HomeKeyService's
#   boot check, #1116). On the real remote Home, YouTube (190), Netflix (191), the mic, holding Home (quick
#   settings) and holding Volume Up (kept ramping) all worked. The Cast receiver was running; casting from a phone
#   was not tried after the launcher went in.
# - Private DNS (dns.adguard-dns.com) broke every name lookup while the Tailscale app's VPN was up: `ping`
#   answered "unknown host", while 8.8.8.8 and port 853 on the resolver answered. Reverted; not tried with
#   Tailscale off.
